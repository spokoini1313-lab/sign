FAQ — Smart Pricing & Profit Calculator (ContractorOS)
=======================================================

Q: Do I need Excel, or will Google Sheets work?
A: Both. Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive and open with Google
Sheets — all formulas and formatting carry over.

Q: I'm not good with Excel. Is this hard to use?
A: No. You only ever type into the yellow cells. Everything else
calculates itself. A Quick Start tab walks you through it in 5 steps.

Q: Can I add more rows / more jobs?
A: Yes — copy any existing row (including its formulas) and paste it
below the table. The formulas adjust automatically.

Q: How is the "recommended price" calculated?
A: Minimum Price = Total Cost ÷ (1 − Target Profit %). This guarantees
your target profit as a percentage of the final price, not just a markup
over cost — which is a much safer way to price. It's then rounded up to
the nearest 10 for a clean quote number.

Q: What's the difference between Margin and Markup?
A: Margin = profit ÷ selling price. Markup = profit ÷ cost. They are
easy to confuse and give very different numbers on the same job — the
sheet calculates both so you always know which one you're looking at.

Q: Can I delete the example data?
A: Yes. Select rows 4–8 in Main Workspace and delete their contents (or
delete the rows). Full instructions are in Quick Start.

Q: Does this include invoicing or sending quotes to clients?
A: This product is a pricing & profitability calculator. Quote building
and sending is handled by our companion product, Smart Quote Builder
(Product 3) — available separately or as part of the ContractorOS bundle.

Q: Will this work on my phone?
A: Excel and Google Sheets mobile apps can open the file, but for the
best experience (dashboard visibility, entering data comfortably) a
laptop, tablet, or desktop is recommended.

Q: Is my data private?
A: Yes. This is a local file — nothing is uploaded or shared unless you
choose to save it to your own cloud storage.
