ContractorOS — Smart Pricing & Profit Calculator
==================================================

WHAT IT IS
A ready-to-use Excel / Google Sheets workbook that calculates the true cost
and correct price of every job you take on — before you send the quote.

WHO IT'S FOR
Electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, painters, carpenters, general
contractors, renovation crews, aluminum/glazing installers, drywall
installers, sealing/waterproofing crews, service technicians, and any
project-based small business owner who prices jobs by hand or by feel.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Most tradespeople price jobs using gut feeling or a rough materials
estimate. Labor, travel, tool wear, overhead, and subcontractor costs get
forgotten — and by the time the job is done, the "profitable" job barely
broke even. This tool forces every real cost onto the table before you
quote, and tells you plainly: profitable, marginal, or unprofitable.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Dashboard with live KPIs: total jobs, total profit, average margin,
  and a breakdown of profitable / marginal / unprofitable jobs
- Main Workspace: one row per job, only yellow cells need filling in
- Automatic calculation of labor cost, direct cost, overhead, total cost,
  minimum price, recommended price (with and without VAT), profit in
  currency, profit margin %, and markup %
- Color-coded status per job (green / yellow / red)
- Business Insights alerts on the Dashboard
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, and full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter number of workers, hourly cost, hours, materials and all
   associated expenses (travel, fuel, parking, subcontractors, equipment,
   tool rental, other).
2. Set your overhead %, target profit %, and VAT %.
3. The sheet calculates your true total cost and the minimum and
   recommended price — automatically, with live formulas.
4. The Dashboard shows you, at a glance, whether your business as a whole
   is pricing profitably.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros, no
add-ins, no installation required.

FORMAT
.xlsx workbook, 4 supporting sheets (Dashboard / Main Workspace /
Quick Start / User Guide), fully formula-driven, zero hardcoded results.
