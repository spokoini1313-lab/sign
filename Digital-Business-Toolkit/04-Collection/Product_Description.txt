ContractorOS — Payment & Collection Manager
==============================================

WHAT IT IS
An Excel/Google Sheets workbook that tracks every invoice, what's been
paid, what's still outstanding, and who is overdue — automatically.

WHO IT'S FOR
Any tradesperson or contractor who bills clients and needs to stay on
top of collections without chasing a stack of paper invoices.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Cash flow problems caused by payments that quietly slip past their due
date because nobody was tracking them closely enough.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main Workspace: one row per invoice — client, project, invoice number,
  issue date, amount, amount paid, due date
- Automatic balance and status calculation (paid / open / partially paid
  / overdue) based on today's date vs. the due date
- Dashboard with live KPIs: total billing, total paid, outstanding
  balance, overdue count, collection rate
- Color-coded status, Business Insights alerts
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add one row per invoice as you issue it.
2. Update "Amount Paid" as payments come in.
3. Balance and status update automatically — including flipping to
   "Overdue" the moment today passes the due date, if unpaid.
4. Dashboard shows your full collections picture at a glance.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros required.
