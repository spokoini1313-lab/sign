FAQ — Payment & Collection Manager (ContractorOS)
====================================================

Q: How does "Overdue" get calculated?
A: Automatically — if there's still a balance owed and today's date is
past the due date, the row flips to "Overdue."

Q: What if a client pays part of the invoice?
A: Enter the partial amount in "Amount Paid" — the balance updates and
status shows "Partially Paid" (or "Overdue" if also past due).

Q: Can I track multiple invoices per project?
A: Yes — just add one row per invoice; they all share the same Project
name so you can filter/sort by project.

Q: Does this send payment reminders to clients?
A: No — this is a tracking tool. Use the Dashboard's overdue count and
alerts to know exactly who to follow up with.

Q: Can I change the due-date payment terms per client?
A: Yes — the due date is entered per invoice, so terms can vary freely.
