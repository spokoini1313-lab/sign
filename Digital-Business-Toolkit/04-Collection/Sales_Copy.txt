ContractorOS — Payment & Collection Manager
==============================================

HEADLINE
Stop losing track of who owes you money.

SUBHEADLINE
Every invoice, every payment, every overdue balance — tracked
automatically, with a status that flips to "overdue" the moment a
client misses their due date.

small unpaid balances nobody was watching closely enough.

✔ Hebrew interface, right-to-left, built for Israeli tradespeople
✔ Works on Excel, Excel Online, and Google Sheets

WHO IT'S FOR
Every trade that bills clients: electricians, plumbers, HVAC, painters,
carpenters, renovation contractors.

CALL TO ACTION
