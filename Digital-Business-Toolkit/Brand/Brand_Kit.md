# Brand Kit — Digital Business Toolkit for Contractors & Tradespeople

## 1. Name Candidates (10)

1. **ProCraft Control** — professional + craft trades
2. **TradeLedger** — accounting/ledger feel for trades
3. **JobMargin** — direct reference to profit per job
4. **ContractorOS** — positions the product as an operating system, SaaS-ready
5. **SiteLedger** — construction site + bookkeeping
6. **MarginWorks** — profit-focused, workshop feel
7. **TradeBase** — simple, international, SaaS-ready
8. **CrewControl** — team/crew management angle
9. **ProfitSite** — profit + construction site
10. **BuildLedger** — construction + financial control

## 2. Top 3 Selected

### 🥇 1. ContractorOS
- **Tagline:** "The operating system for your contracting business."
- **Brand Meaning:** Positions the toolkit as core infrastructure, not just spreadsheets — implies a full system that runs the business, and scales naturally into a future SaaS product without a rename.
- **Domain Ideas:** contractoros.com / getcontractoros.com / contractoros.io / contractor-os.com
- **Visual Direction:** Dark navy (#17365D) + professional blue (#1F4E78), squared-off geometric mark suggesting a control panel/dashboard grid, monospace accents on numbers to feel "systemized," high-contrast white space.

### 🥈 2. JobMargin
- **Tagline:** "Know your number on every job."
- **Brand Meaning:** Speaks directly to the #1 pain point — contractors who don't know if a job is actually profitable. Sharp, benefit-first name that a non-technical tradesperson understands in one read.
- **Domain Ideas:** jobmargin.com / getjobmargin.com / jobmargin.io
- **Visual Direction:** Bold blue/green duotone, large numeral motifs (%, ₪, $), clean sans-serif, "before/after" profit bar visuals on marketing material.

### 🥉 3. TradeBase
- **Tagline:** "Every job. Every number. One base."
- **Brand Meaning:** Neutral, international-friendly, easy to say in Hebrew and English, works equally well as a spreadsheet toolkit today and a SaaS platform tomorrow.
- **Domain Ideas:** tradebase.com (likely taken) / usetradebase.com / tradebase.io / trade-base.com
- **Visual Direction:** Flat professional palette, grid/foundation iconography (a base plate icon), confident and neutral — least "flashy" of the three, most "enterprise-ready."

**Recommendation: ContractorOS** — strongest long-term brand equity, most credible if/when this becomes a SaaS product, and the name alone signals "this replaces your scattered tools," which is the core promise of Product 11.

All product files, covers, and copy in this build use **ContractorOS** as the working brand name. `BRAND_NAME` and `SALES_EMAIL` are the only two values that need to change if you rename or relaunch.

## 3. Visual Identity

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Dark Navy | `#17365D` | Headers, primary text on light, sidebar/nav |
| Professional Blue | `#1F4E78` | Primary buttons, links, active states |
| Light Blue | `#D9EAF7` | Section backgrounds, table header fill |
| Success Green | `#E2F0D9` | "OK / Profitable / On Track" status fill |
| Input Yellow | `#FFF2CC` | Editable input cells (always) |
| Alert Red | `#F4CCCC` | "Problem / Overdue / Over Budget" status fill |
| Light Background | `#F8FAFC` | Page/sheet background |

**Typography:** Heebo or Assistant (Hebrew-native, clean, professional) for UI; Arial/Calibri fallback inside Excel (font embedding isn't portable, so Excel files use **Calibri** for maximum compatibility, with Heebo reserved for the landing page and PDFs).

**Tone of voice:** Direct, respectful, non-corporate. Speaks to a tradesperson as a peer running a real business — never condescending, never "startup-speak."

**Logo concept:** Wordmark "ContractorOS" in Calibri/Heebo Bold, Dark Navy, with a small squared bracket `[ ]` motif around the "OS" suggesting a system/interface — reproducible as pure text (no image dependency) for use inside Excel headers.
