ContractorOS — Smart Quote Builder
=====================================

WHAT IT IS
An Excel/Google Sheets workbook for building clean, professional,
multi-line-item price quotes — with automatic totals, discounts, and VAT.

WHO IT'S FOR
Any tradesperson or contractor who sends price quotes to clients:
electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs, painters, carpenters, renovation
contractors, and subcontractors of every trade.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Quotes built from scratch every time, in inconsistent formats, with
manual (error-prone) totals and forgotten VAT or discounts — which look
unprofessional and cost you client trust.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main Workspace: one row per line item (work description, unit,
  quantity, unit price, discount %) grouped by Quote Number
- Quote Summary: one row per quote, with validity, payment terms, VAT %,
  and automatically calculated subtotal / VAT / total
- Dashboard with live KPIs: total quotes, total quoted value, approved
  quotes, approval rate, average quote value
- Status tracking (draft / sent / approved / rejected) with color coding
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add one row per work item to Main Workspace, tagged with a Quote Number.
2. Add one row per quote to Quote Summary with terms and VAT.
3. Totals calculate automatically from the line items.
4. Filter to one quote and print/export to PDF for the client.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros required.
