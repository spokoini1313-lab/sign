ContractorOS — Cash Flow Planner
===================================

WHAT IT IS
A 12-month cash flow planner that tracks opening balance, expected and
actual income/expenses, monthly net, and closing balance — with a live
chart and automatic cash-runway calculation.

WHO IT'S FOR
Contractors and small business owners who need to see, months in
advance, whether they're heading toward a cash crunch.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Cash flow surprises — discovering there isn't enough cash to cover an
expense only when the bill is due, instead of months earlier when there
was still time to act.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main Workspace: pre-built 12-month table, opening balance carries
  forward automatically month to month
- Automatic net and closing-balance calculation
- Monthly balance trend chart
- Dashboard with live KPIs: current closing balance, average monthly
  net, months in deficit, lowest balance point, Cash Runway (months)
- Color-coded status, Business Insights alerts
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your opening balance once, for month 1.
2. Fill in expected and actual income/expenses for each month.
3. Net and closing balance calculate and carry forward automatically.
4. The Dashboard tells you your runway and flags any month at risk.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros required.
