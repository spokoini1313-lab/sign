ContractorOS — Cash Flow Planner
===================================

HEADLINE
See a cash crunch coming — months before it happens.

SUBHEADLINE
A 12-month cash flow planner that carries your balance forward
automatically and tells you exactly how many months your cash will last.

WHY YOU NEED THIS
Most cash flow problems aren't a mystery in hindsight — they were
visible months ahead, if anyone had been tracking the trend. This tool
makes that trend impossible to miss.

WHAT YOU GET
✔ A pre-built 12-month cash flow table — just fill in the numbers
✔ Automatic balance carry-forward, net, and closing balance
✔ A live monthly balance trend chart
✔ Cash Runway calculation — how many months you can sustain your burn rate
✔ Hebrew interface, right-to-left, built for Israeli tradespeople
✔ Works on Excel, Excel Online, and Google Sheets

WHO IT'S FOR
Any project-based small business owner who wants to plan cash, not just
react to it.

CALL TO ACTION
Get Cash Flow Planner and always know where your cash stands.
