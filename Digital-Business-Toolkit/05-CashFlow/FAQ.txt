FAQ — Cash Flow Planner (ContractorOS)
=========================================

Q: Do I need to enter opening balance every month?
A: No — only for the first month. Every following month pulls its
opening balance automatically from the prior month's closing balance.

Q: What is "Cash Runway"?
A: How many months your current closing balance would last if your
average monthly net (income minus expenses) continued at its current
pace. Shows "∞" when your average net is positive or breakeven.

Q: Can I plan more than one year?
A: Yes — copy the 12-month block below the existing one; the new
January's opening balance should reference the prior December's closing.

Q: What if I don't have "actual" numbers yet for future months?
A: Leave them at 0 for now — the sheet will simply show 0 net for that
month until you update it.

Q: Does the chart update automatically?
A: Yes — it's linked directly to the closing balance column.
