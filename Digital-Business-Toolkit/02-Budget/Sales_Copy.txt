ContractorOS — Project Budget Controller
==========================================

HEADLINE
Catch a budget overrun while you can still fix it.

SUBHEADLINE
Track planned vs. actual spend on every project, by category, with
automatic variance alerts — so you find out about an overrun this week,
not after the job wraps.

WHY YOU NEED THIS
Materials cost more than planned. A subcontractor runs over. Overhead
creeps. By the time you tally it all up at the end, the damage is done.
This tool flags it category-by-category, in real time.

WHAT YOU GET
✔ A professional Excel/Google Sheets budget tracker — ready in minutes
✔ Automatic variance % and status per category
✔ A live Dashboard showing total overrun and utilization across all projects
✔ Color-coded status: on budget / watch / overrun
✔ Hebrew interface, right-to-left, built for Israeli tradespeople
✔ Works on Excel, Excel Online, and Google Sheets

WHO IT'S FOR
Renovation contractors • Electricians • Plumbers • HVAC crews • General
contractors managing several jobs at once

CALL TO ACTION
Get Project Budget Controller and stop finding out about overruns too late.
