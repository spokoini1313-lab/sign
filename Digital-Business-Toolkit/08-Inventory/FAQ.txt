FAQ — Inventory & Materials Manager (ContractorOS)
=====================================================

Q: How is "Low Stock" determined?
A: Automatically — when quantity is at or below the minimum stock level
you set for that item.

Q: What if quantity hits zero?
A: The status automatically switches to "Out of Stock."

Q: Can I organize items by category?
A: Yes — pick from a built-in category dropdown (electrical, plumbing,
HVAC, drywall, paint, aluminum, carpentry, sealing, tools, other), or
sort/filter by category.

Q: How do I update quantity after using or receiving stock?
A: Simply edit the Quantity cell — stock value and status recalculate
automatically.

Q: Does this track which project used which materials?
A: Not directly — this product tracks overall stock levels. For
per-project material cost, log the purchase in Project Budget Controller
(Product 2) under the "Materials" category.
