ContractorOS — Inventory & Materials Manager
================================================

WHAT IT IS
An Excel/Google Sheets inventory tracker for materials and tools, with
automatic stock value and low-stock/out-of-stock alerts.

WHO IT'S FOR
Contractors and tradespeople who keep a materials or tool stock — in a
warehouse, a van, or a storage room — and don't want to discover a
shortage mid-job.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Arriving on site and finding a critical material has run out — a full
lost work day, or an emergency supplier run at a markup.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main Workspace: one row per item — SKU, name, category, unit,
  quantity, minimum stock, unit cost, supplier
- Automatic stock value calculation (quantity × unit cost)
- Automatic status: OK / Low Stock - Reorder / Out of Stock
- Dashboard with live KPIs: total inventory value, item count, low-stock
  count, out-of-stock count, highest-value item
- Color-coded status, Business Insights alerts
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Log every stock item with current quantity and your minimum
   threshold.
2. Stock value and status calculate automatically.
3. The Dashboard flags anything at or below minimum, or fully out.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros required.
