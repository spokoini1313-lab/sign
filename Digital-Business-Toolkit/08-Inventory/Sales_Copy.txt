ContractorOS — Inventory & Materials Manager
================================================

HEADLINE
Never run out of material mid-job again.

SUBHEADLINE
Track every item's stock level, value, and reorder point automatically
— and get flagged the moment something drops below minimum.

WHY YOU NEED THIS
Running out of a critical material on site costs you a day — or forces
an expensive emergency purchase. This tool tells you before it happens.

WHAT YOU GET
✔ A professional Excel/Google Sheets inventory tracker
✔ Automatic stock value and reorder-status calculation
✔ A live Dashboard: total inventory value, low stock, out of stock
✔ Color-coded status: OK / low stock / out of stock
✔ Hebrew interface, right-to-left, built for Israeli tradespeople
✔ Works on Excel, Excel Online, and Google Sheets

WHO IT'S FOR
Any contractor or tradesperson who keeps materials or tools in stock.

CALL TO ACTION
Get Inventory & Materials Manager and stop losing work days to a
material shortage.
