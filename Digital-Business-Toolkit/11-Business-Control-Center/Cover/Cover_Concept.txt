Product Cover Concept — מרכז הבקרה העסקי (Product 11 — Premium)
====================================================================
STYLE: Premium, clean, construction-business, modern SaaS — visually
the most premium of the 11 covers, signaling this is the flagship /
Ultimate product.

LAYOUT (1200x1500 portrait): Dark Navy full background (not split like
the other 10 — full-bleed navy signals premium tier). Gold/light-blue
accent line under the wordmark. "[ContractorOS]" wordmark top-left,
slightly larger than other covers. Product name center, white bold
44px: "מרכז הבקרה העסקי" with English subtitle "Business Control
Center" smaller beneath. Tagline: "כל העסק שלכם, במבט אחד."
Middle: a composite mock dashboard — 4 small KPI tiles arranged in a
grid (revenue, margin, cash, active projects), suggesting it aggregates
everything the other 10 covers show individually. Bottom: 10 small
numbered dots/icons (1-10) converging into a single "11" badge in
Professional Blue — visually saying "all 10 products become 1."
Badge: "Excel + Google Sheets — המוצר המלא".

COLOR USE: Dark Navy #17365D dominant · Professional Blue #1F4E78 ·
Light Blue #D9EAF7 accents only.

DELIVERABLES: Square 1000x1000, Portrait 1200x1500, Wide 1600x900.
