METHOD
Same limitation as the rest of this build: automated LibreOffice
recalculation is unavailable in this sandbox (`soffice` cannot load any
file here — see `01-Pricing/QA_Notes.txt` for the full diagnosis). This
workbook was verified manually instead:

- Every sheet's formulas re-read via openpyxl and checked cell-by-cell:
  Projects (overrun %, balance, profit, margin, risk classification),
  Cash Flow (opening-balance carry-forward, net, closing balance —
  same verified pattern as Product 5), Inventory (stock value — same
  pattern as Product 8), Labor (input-only, no formulas needed).
- Executive Dashboard KPI formulas cross-checked against the correct
  sheet name and range for each of the 12 cards (SUM/AVERAGE/COUNTIF/
  COUNTIFS against Projects, Sales & Leads, Cash Flow, Inventory,
  Labor).
- Business Insights formulas (the most complex in this build — nested
  IF + INDEX/MATCH/MAX to name the worst-overrun and most-profitable
  project by name) traced by hand against the 5 example projects:
  confirmed MAX() correctly ignores the blank extra rows (which return
  "" via their own IF guard, and text is excluded from MAX), and that
  each COUNTIFS/COUNTIF gate correctly falls back to the "no issue"
  message when its condition count is 0.
- Only pre-2007 Excel functions used (SUM, AVERAGE, COUNTA, COUNTIF,
  COUNTIFS, INDEX, MATCH, MAX, IF, IFERROR, TEXT, string concatenation)
  — no XLOOKUP/FILTER/UNIQUE/SEQUENCE, no post-2007 functions.
- Cross-product name-key verified: this product's 5 example projects
  use the exact same names, spelled identically, as Products 1, 2, 4,
  6, 9, and 10 — confirmed by direct comparison against each product's
  example data.
- RTL confirmed on every sheet.

DESIGN NOTE — why Product 11 has its own data entry rather than live
links to the other 10 files
Excel/Google Sheets cross-file references are fragile in exactly the
way this build's tooling warns about (external links break on resave,
require the other file to be present on disk, and don't survive being
distributed as separate marketplace products people buy independently).
Product 11 is therefore a self-contained workbook: it has its own
condensed Projects / Sales & Leads / Cash Flow / Inventory / Labor
sheets that the user updates with a summary from whichever of the 10
individual products they also own, on a recommended weekly cadence.
This is documented plainly in Product 11's own Quick Start, User Guide,
and FAQ so it's not a surprise to a buyer who expects "automatic"
syncing between separately-purchased files.
