ContractorOS — Contractor CRM
================================

WHAT IT IS
A lead- and client-tracking workbook built for how contractors actually
sell: phone calls, WhatsApp, a site visit, a quote, then a yes or no.

WHO IT'S FOR
Any tradesperson or contractor who gets inbound leads (referrals, social
media, Google, signage) and wants to stop losing track of them.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Leads that fall through the cracks because there's no simple system
tracking who to follow up with, and when.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main Workspace: one row per lead — contact info, source, job type,
  area, potential value, pipeline stage, lead temperature, next follow-up
- 7-stage pipeline: New Lead → Contacted → Meeting → Quote Sent →
  Negotiation → Won → Lost
- Dashboard with live KPIs: total leads, conversion rate, total
  potential revenue, won revenue, open hot leads
- Color-coded stage and temperature, follow-up alerts
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Log every new lead as it comes in.
2. Update its pipeline stage and temperature as it progresses.
3. Set a next follow-up date — the Dashboard flags anything due today or overdue.
4. Track conversion rate and revenue won automatically.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros required.
