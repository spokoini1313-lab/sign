FAQ — Contractor CRM (ContractorOS)
======================================

Q: What are the pipeline stages?
A: New Lead, Contacted, Meeting Scheduled, Quote Sent, Negotiation, Won,
Lost — pick from a dropdown in the Status column.

Q: What does "Lead Temperature" mean?
A: A quick read of urgency/interest: Hot, Warm, Cold — helps you decide
who to call first.

Q: How does the Dashboard know which follow-ups are due?
A: It compares each lead's "Next Follow-Up" date to today's date and
counts anything due or overdue automatically.

Q: Can I track where my leads come from?
A: Yes — the Source column (referral, Facebook, Instagram, Google,
WhatsApp, signage, repeat client, other) lets you filter or count by
source to see what's working.

Q: Does closing a lead update revenue automatically?
A: Yes — mark it "Won" and its potential amount counts automatically
toward "Won Revenue" on the Dashboard.
