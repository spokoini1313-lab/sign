ContractorOS — Project Execution Dashboard
==============================================

HEADLINE
Run every project on schedule, not on memory.

SUBHEADLINE
Break any project into tasks, assign owners, and let this tool tell you
automatically what's overdue and how close you are to done.

WHY YOU NEED THIS
A project falls behind one small slipped task at a time — and by the
time it's obvious, you're already explaining a delay to the client.
This tool surfaces slippage the day it happens.

WHAT YOU GET
✔ A professional Excel/Google Sheets task tracker
✔ Automatic days-remaining and overdue detection
✔ A live Dashboard: total/completed/in-progress/overdue tasks, completion %
✔ Color-coded status and urgency
✔ Hebrew interface, right-to-left, built for Israeli tradespeople
✔ Works on Excel, Excel Online, and Google Sheets

WHO IT'S FOR
Any contractor managing multi-stage projects with several people
responsible for different parts.

CALL TO ACTION
Get Project Execution Dashboard and always know exactly where the
project stands.
