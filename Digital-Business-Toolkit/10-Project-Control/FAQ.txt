FAQ — Project Execution Dashboard (ContractorOS)
===================================================

Q: How is "Days Remaining" calculated?
A: Due Date minus today's date. A negative number means the task is
overdue; the cell turns red automatically.

Q: What happens once a task is marked "Completed" or "Canceled"?
A: Days Remaining shows "-" instead of a number, since it's no longer
relevant to track.

Q: Can I manage more than one project in this file?
A: Yes — add your own Project column if you're tracking several projects
at once, or keep one workbook copy per project for a cleaner view.

Q: How is overall Project Completion % calculated?
A: It's the average of every task's individual Completion % — so
weight tasks by breaking large ones into smaller, evenly-sized tasks if
you want a more accurate average.

Q: What does the Dashboard's "Nearest Deadline" show?
A: The closest due date among all tasks that aren't yet completed or
canceled — the next thing you genuinely need to worry about.
