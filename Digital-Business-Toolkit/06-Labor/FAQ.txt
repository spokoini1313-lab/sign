FAQ — Employee & Labor Cost Tracker (ContractorOS)
=====================================================

Q: How is overtime calculated?
A: Automatically — any hours beyond 8 in a single row (one work day for
one employee) count as overtime, multiplied by your overtime multiplier
(default 1.5x).

Q: What if an employee worked on two projects in one day?
A: Add two separate rows for that day, splitting the actual hours
between the two Project IDs.

Q: Can I see labor cost per project?
A: Yes — filter or SUMIF the "Daily Cost" column by Project ID. Product
11 (Business Control Center) rolls this up automatically across all
modules.

Q: What if the standard 8-hour threshold doesn't match my business?
A: Edit the "8" in the Regular/Overtime formulas to your own threshold.

Q: Does this handle payroll or tax deductions?
A: No — this is a cost-tracking tool for project profitability, not a
payroll system.
