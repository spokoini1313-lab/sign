ContractorOS — Employee & Labor Cost Tracker
================================================

WHAT IT IS
A daily hours and labor-cost tracker that calculates regular hours,
overtime, and true daily labor cost per employee per project —
automatically, from start and end time.

WHO IT'S FOR
Contractors and tradespeople who manage a crew — even a small one — and
need to know what labor actually costs, project by project.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Not knowing the real labor cost of a project until long after it's
finished — overtime especially tends to disappear into a vague sense
that "we worked a lot," without ever being tied to a number.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main Workspace: one row per employee per work day — date, employee,
  project, start/end time, hourly rate, overtime multiplier
- Automatic regular-hours (up to 8/day) and overtime-hours calculation
- Automatic daily labor cost calculation
- Dashboard with live KPIs: total hours, total labor cost, total
  overtime, average hourly cost, logged work-days
- Color-coded overtime flags, Business Insights alerts
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add a row per employee per day, with start and end time.
2. Set the hourly rate and overtime multiplier (default 1.5x).
3. Regular hours, overtime hours, and daily cost calculate automatically.
4. Use the shared Project ID to tie labor cost back to a specific job.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros required.
