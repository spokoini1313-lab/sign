ContractorOS — Employee & Labor Cost Tracker
================================================

HEADLINE
Know exactly what your crew costs — every day, every project.

SUBHEADLINE
Log start and end times and this tool calculates regular hours,
overtime, and true daily labor cost automatically.

WHY YOU NEED THIS
Overtime quietly eats margin. Without a system, you only feel it at the
end of the month — this tool shows it to you the day it happens.

WHAT YOU GET
✔ A professional Excel/Google Sheets labor tracker
✔ Automatic regular/overtime hours and daily cost calculation
✔ A live Dashboard: total hours, total labor cost, overtime, avg cost
✔ Color-coded overtime flags
✔ Hebrew interface, right-to-left, built for Israeli tradespeople
✔ Works on Excel, Excel Online, and Google Sheets

WHO IT'S FOR
Any contractor or tradesperson managing a crew, even a small one.

CALL TO ACTION
Get Employee & Labor Cost Tracker and know your true labor cost, every day.
