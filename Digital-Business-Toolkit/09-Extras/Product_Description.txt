ContractorOS — Change Order & Extras Manager
================================================

WHAT IT IS
A tracker for every "extra" or change request a client makes beyond the
original quote — from approval through execution, billing, and payment.

WHO IT'S FOR
Any contractor whose clients ask for "one more thing" mid-project — which
is to say, almost every contractor.

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Verbal change requests that get done, forgotten, and never billed —
quietly eating your margin on an otherwise profitable job.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Main Workspace: one row per extra — date, project, client,
  description, quantity, unit price, client approval, execution status,
  billed?, paid?
- Automatic line total calculation
- Dashboard with live KPIs: total extras value, approved value, pending
  approval, completed-but-unbilled value, billed-but-unpaid value
- Color-coded approval status, Business Insights alerts
- Full Hebrew RTL interface, Quick Start guide, full User Guide with FAQ

HOW IT WORKS
1. Log every change request the moment it's discussed.
2. Track client approval before work begins.
3. Update execution, billing, and payment status as the extra progresses.
4. The Dashboard catches anything completed but not yet billed.

COMPATIBILITY
Excel 2016+ (Windows/Mac), Excel Online, Google Sheets. No macros required.
