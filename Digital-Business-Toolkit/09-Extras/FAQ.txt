FAQ — Change Order & Extras Manager (ContractorOS)
=====================================================

Q: Should I log a change request before or after client approval?
A: Log it immediately, then track approval status separately — that way
nothing gets forgotten while you're waiting on a "yes."

Q: What's the difference between "Billed" and "Paid"?
A: Billed = you issued an invoice/payment request for it. Paid = the
money actually arrived. Both are tracked independently.

Q: How does this connect to the original project?
A: Via the Project ID column — the same ID used across Budget, Quote,
Collection, Labor, and other ContractorOS products.

Q: What's the most important Dashboard number to check regularly?
A: "Completed Not Billed" — that's real, finished work sitting unbilled,
which is the number one way extras quietly cost you money.

Q: Can I track rejected change requests too?
A: Yes — mark Client Approval as "Rejected" to keep a record without it
counting toward billable totals.
